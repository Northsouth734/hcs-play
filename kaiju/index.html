How to play

Left thumb: drag on the left to walk.
Tap ⚡: stomp. At bright red (💥) a tap is a Big Tantrum.
Hold ⚡, drag to aim, let go when the ring meets the circle: fling energy at a target. Green ring = perfect.
Hold ⚡, slide onto 🌬 (next to the button), let go when it flashes pink: breathe and calm down.

Your monster's size and colour are its energy. Calm (teal) is precise, hot (red) is powerful but slippery. Too much energy and it runs wild for a moment: steer it!
